# How to cite ACeT

## Article: method and scientific results

Sabitoj Singh Virk and Akashdeep Singh Virk. **Monoclonal Antibody Developability from Early Assay Panels: Machine Learning for Formulation and Pharmacokinetic Risk.** *Machine Learning: Health*.

The authors contributed equally. Use the publisher's final article DOI and bibliographic details when available; these have not been assigned in this repository's citation metadata.

## Software and data: the version you used

Sabitoj Singh Virk and Akashdeep Singh Virk. **ACeT — antibody developability data and software.** https://github.com/khalsa-singh/ACeT. Include the release tag, archived release DOI or full commit identifier used for your work.

The article and the software archive are different research objects. Cite the article when discussing ACeT's approach or results; also identify the software/data version when reusing or extending the implementation. An article DOI and a software-release DOI should not be interchanged.

## Machine-readable citation

`CITATION.cff` is the standard machine-readable filename. Its top-level title identifies the software/data repository; its `preferred-citation` contains the exact article title. GitHub can display the article as the preferred citation while retaining software metadata for tools.

## Underlying measurements

The repository contains derivatives of the published antibody datasets identified in [Data/SOURCES.md](Data/SOURCES.md). Cite the relevant original studies as well when using their measurements. Software citation does not replace data attribution.
