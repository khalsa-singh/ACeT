Figure 1. Architecture of the agnostic context-embedding transformer (ACeT) for integrated assay-based developability prediction. a, Each assay readout is encoded as the sum of a value embedding and a learned feature-identity embedding, yielding one token per measurement. b, A pre-normalization transformer encoder applies multi-head self-attention and a GELU feed-forward network, each with a residual connection. Global-average pooling then produces a pooled representation. c, Candidate prediction heads are evaluated separately by development cross-validation. The selected head is coupled to a linear regression or softmax classification output for high-concentration viscosity, mouse intravenous exposure, HIC retention time, or clinical outcome classification (Approved vs Terminated). KAN is highlighted only to illustrate the selection route; the selected head is endpoint-specific.
